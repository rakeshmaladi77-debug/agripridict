**Headline: Why I built an AI that learns from its own mistakes (for farmers) 🌾🧠**

Farmers don't need more data—they need **decisions**. 

Most agricultural apps show you prices and weather, but they leave the hard part to the farmer: *"Should I sell today or wait?"*

Over the last few weeks, I’ve been building **AgriPredict**, an adaptive intelligence platform designed to bridge this gap. 🚀

**The Challenge:** Market data is noisy. A sudden rainstorm or a random price dip shouldn't cause an AI to panic. 

**The Solution:** I built a **closed-loop feedback system**. 
- It predicts market trends.
- It tracks the real-world outcome.
- It **learns**. 

Using a stabilized window-based tuning algorithm, the engine automatically re-tunes its weights (e.g., shifting importance from "Weather" to "Market Trends") when it detects a drop in accuracy. 📉➡️📈

**Key Technical Highlights:**
✅ **Stabilized Learning Engine**: Adaptive logic with guardrails to prevent overfitting.
✅ **Explainable AI**: A "Reasoning Block" so users know *why* a decision was made.
✅ **Voice Assistant**: Natural language support for Hinglish, Telugu, and English.
✅ **Visual Evolution**: Live charts showing the model getting smarter over time.

This project taught me that the most powerful AI isn't the one with the most complex math—it's the one that earns **user trust** through transparency and stability.
